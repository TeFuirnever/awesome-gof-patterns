Each design pattern is a three-part description of a design problem, its solution,
and the consequences of applying that solution. The pattern identifies the classes
and objects participating in the design, their roles, and the ways they collaborate.
---
The Strategy pattern defines a family of algorithms, encapsulates each one, and makes
them interchangeable. Strategy lets the algorithm vary independently from clients
that use it. The important thing is that the algorithm can vary without the client
knowing or caring.
